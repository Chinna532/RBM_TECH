How long does it take to deploy a custom web application?

Corporate websites typically take 1 to 2 weeks, while custom Node.js applications or booking engines take 3 to 5 weeks depending on requirements.

Can RBM Technology set up automated WhatsApp and Email receipts?

Yes! We integrate Twilio and Nodemailer directly with your website form submissions to dispatch real-time alerts to customers and admins.

Every platform engineered by RBM Technology uses responsive CSS architecture, ensuring seamless layout adjustment on mobile phones, tablets, and desktops.
